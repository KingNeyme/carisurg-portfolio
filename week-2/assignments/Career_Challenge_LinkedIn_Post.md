## First LinkedIn Post Draft

```text
I have started the CariSurg Healthcare AI Programme, where I am building practical skills at the intersection of healthcare, data, and artificial intelligence.

In the early assignments, I worked with emergency triage data and practised cleaning clinical variables, checking vital-sign ranges, documenting decisions, and creating visualisations that answer clinical questions.

One lesson that stood out to me is that clinical AI starts before modelling. The data must be understood, cleaned responsibly, and explained clearly before it can support any decision-making system.

I am excited to keep developing as an AI engineer and to explore how responsible healthcare technology can support Caribbean clinical environments.

#HealthcareAI #ClinicalAI #MedTech #AIEngineering #CariSurg
```
