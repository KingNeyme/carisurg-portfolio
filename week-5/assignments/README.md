# Week 5 Assignments

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `Week5_AI_Assisted_Triage_Data_Exploration.ipynb` | Week-specific copy of the full exploration notebook |

## Notes For Reviewers

The patient-level raw and cleaned datasets are not committed to Git. They are stored locally under `data/raw/` and `data/processed/`, both of which are ignored for privacy and repository-size reasons.
